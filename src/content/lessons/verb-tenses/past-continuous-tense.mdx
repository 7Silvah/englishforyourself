In this section you will learn information about the **different uses** of the past continuous / progressive tense with examples.

## Uses of the Past Continuous Tense

For a past action that was happening at a specific time.

<Examples>

- I was playing videogames last night.
- Simon’s dad was watering the plants at 7 o’clock.
- At 5 o’clock this morning, it was snowing.

</Examples>

To talk about two actions that were happening at the same time in the past.

<Examples>

- While I was watering the plants, she was feeding the dogs.
- While Megan was singing, we were dancing.
- They were playing videogames while I was doing homework.

</Examples>

For an action that was happening (past continuous) and was interrupted by another action (past simple).

<Examples>

- I was cleaning the kitchen when I heard something.
- Kate was playing with her cat when her mom called her.
- We were having breakfast when my cousins arrived.

</Examples>

To give background information about someone or something in a narrative.

<Examples>

- Jim was at the park. People were having picnics and their kids were playing and running around.
- As the sun was setting, many people gathered to take photographs of the sky.
- The concert was about to start. Fans were singing and enjoying themselves.

</Examples>
