## Explicación

Cuando un verbo va detrás de otro, el segundo puede ir en **-ing** (el **gerundio**:
*reading*) o en **to + verbo** (el **infinitivo**: *to read*). Depende del primer verbo,
y hay que aprenderlo con él.

### Verbo + -ing

*enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, keep, practise, miss, can't stand*:

<Formula parts={["subj:Sujeto", "verb:enjoy / finish…", "obj:verbo + -ing"]} example={["I", "enjoy", "reading."]} />

<Examples>

- She **finished writing** the report.
- Do you **mind waiting** a moment?
- I **avoid driving** at night.

</Examples>

### Verbo + to + verbo

*want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, learn, afford, would like*:

<Formula parts={["subj:Sujeto", "verb:want / decide…", "obj:to + verbo"]} example={["We", "decided", "to stay."]} />

<Examples>

- I **want to learn** Japanese.
- They **can't afford to buy** a house.
- He **promised to call** me.

</Examples>

### Con los dos, casi sin diferencia

*like, love, hate, start, begin*: *I like **swimming*** = *I like **to swim***. *It started
**raining*** = *It started **to rain***.

### Siempre -ing

- **Después de una preposición** (*in, at, of, about, before, after, without…*):
  *I'm interested **in learning** Italian.* · *She left **without saying** goodbye.*
- **Como sujeto**: ***Swimming** is good for you.*

## Comparación con el español

<Explain>

El español usa el **infinitivo** en muchos casos en que el inglés pide **-ing**:

- *Nadar es sano* → **Swimming is healthy** (no *Swim is healthy*).
- *Antes de salir* → **before leaving** (no *before to leave*).
- *Gracias por venir* → **Thanks for coming**.

Y *quiero que vengas* no lleva *that*: **I want you to come**.

</Explain>

## Errores comunes

<CommonMistake wrong="I enjoy to read." right="I enjoy reading.">
Enjoy va con -ing.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="I want going home." right="I want to go home.">
Want va con to + verbo.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="She left without say goodbye." right="She left without saying goodbye.">
Después de una preposición (without), -ing.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="I want that you come." right="I want you to come.">
Want + persona + to + verbo, sin that.
</CommonMistake>

<Tip>

Truco: con los verbos de **disfrutar o terminar** algo que ya haces (*enjoy, finish, keep*)
suele ir **-ing**; con los de **planes y deseos** hacia el futuro (*want, hope, plan,
decide*), **to**.

</Tip>
