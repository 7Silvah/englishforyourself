## Explicación

El pasado de *to be* tiene dos formas:

- **was** con *I, he, she, it*
- **were** con *you, we, they*

<Formula parts={["subj:Sujeto", "verb:was / were", "obj:Complemento"]} example={["She", "was", "at home."]} />

<Modes pos="They were happy." neg="They weren't happy." q="Were they happy?" />

Respuestas cortas: *Was it good? — Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.*

También en pasado: **there was** (una cosa) y **there were** (varias).

### Expresiones de pasado

*yesterday* (ayer), *last night* (anoche), *last week / month / year*, *two days
ago* (hace dos días), *in 2010*.

## Comparación con el español

<Explain>

En español hay muchas formas: *era, estaba, fue, estuvo*. En inglés todas son
**was** o **were**: *Era profesor* → *He was a teacher*; *Estuve en casa* → *I was at home*.

Como en presente, la edad va con to be: *Tenía 10 años* → **I was 10 years old**.

*Había / hubo* se dice **there was / there were**.

</Explain>

## Errores comunes

<CommonMistake wrong="I were at home." right="I was at home.">
Con I, was.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="They was late." right="They were late.">
Con they, were.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="You were tired?" right="Were you tired?">
En la pregunta, was o were va antes del sujeto, como en presente.
</CommonMistake>

<CommonMistake wrong="There were a problem." right="There was a problem.">
Una sola cosa: there was.
</CommonMistake>

<Tip>

**Was** con una persona o cosa (salvo *you*); **were** con *you* y con los plurales.

</Tip>
