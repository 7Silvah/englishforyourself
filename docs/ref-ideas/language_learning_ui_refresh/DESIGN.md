## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for deep focus, structured language learning, and grammatical parsing. Rooted in technical precision, high clarity, and dark-mode ergonomics, it treats linguistic syntax with the visual rigor of code development environments. The target audience comprises language scholars, polyglots, and computational linguistics enthusiasts who value cognitive clarity and sustained visual comfort.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Developer-Tool Ergonomics** with **Data-Dense Syntax Highlighting**:
- **Pure Dark Canvases**: Deep carbon backgrounds reduce eye fatigue during long analytical sessions.
- **Architectonic Surfaces**: Elevated cards and modules delineated by subtle, crisp borders rather than heavy atmospheric drops.
- **Vibrant Functional Accents**: High-contrast, semantic-driven color accents (Cyan/Electric Blue, Amber, Emerald, and Lavender Purple) applied surgically to grammatical roles without overwhelming the layout.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast tiers designed for absolute clarity against pitch-dark backdrops. 

### Surface & Border Structure
- **Base Canvas**: `#0D0F12` (deep obsidian canvas)
- **Container Tiers**:
  - Surface Level 1 (Primary Panels): `#14161B`
  - Surface Level 2 (Elevated Modules & Insets): `#181B22`
  - Surface Level 3 (Hover States & Active Toggles): `#20252F`
- **Structural Outlines**: `#242833` (subtle 1px boundary layer)

### Semantic Syntax Spectrum
Accents follow strict grammatical assignments to enable immediate visual scanning:
- **Primary / Subject**: Deep Electric Blue (`#2563EB`) paired with bright Cyan (`#38BDF8`) for highlights and active focal elements.
- **Auxiliary & Modals**: Warm Gold / Amber (`#D97706` base, `#F59E0B` text and indicators).
- **Predicates & Verbs**: Crisp Emerald (`#10B981` base, `#34D399` text and indicators).
- **Objects & Complements**: Soft Purple / Amethyst (`#8B5CF6` base, `#A78BFA` text and indicators).

### Neutral Typography
- Primary Text: `#F1F5F9` (96% contrast against base)
- Secondary Text: `#94A3B8`
- Muted / Metadata: `#64748B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy distinguishes structured structural prose from parsed syntactic fragments. 

- **Display & Headings (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, crisp geometric sans with refined tracking gives major sections and analytical metrics authority without visual clutter.
- **Interface & Analytical Prose (`Geist`)**: Neutral, sharp, and highly legible body typography designed for dense tabular data and sentence stream rendering.
- **Syntactic Chips, Badges & Phonetics (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Monospaced clarity specifically allocated for grammatical role tags, pronunciation transcriptions, and structural syntax blocks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a strict 12-column fluid grid on desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Modular Syntax Blocks**: Grammar breakdowns rely on flexbox wrapping layouts with uniform horizontal gaps of `space-sm` (0.5rem) and vertical line gaps of `space-md` (1rem) to prevent parsed words from colliding across lines.
- **Card Content Insets**: Standard containers enforce an inner padding of `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop, tapering to `space-md` (1rem) on mobile screens.
- **Breakpoint Scaling**:
  - Desktop (`>= 1024px`): 12 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
  - Tablet (`640px - 1023px`): 8 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`.
  - Mobile (`< 640px`): 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry drop shadows, instead using **surface luminance layering and crisp low-contrast borders**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#0D0F12`.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards)**: Solid `#14161B` surface enveloped by a hairline `1px solid #242833` stroke.
- **Level 2 (Active/Interactive Modules)**: Solid `#181B22` surface with `1px solid #2E3442`. In active state, cards emit a tight, colored outline glow: `0 0 0 1px #2563EB, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Menus & Overlays)**: Surface `#1C2029` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and bound by `1px solid #333A4A`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances architectural precision with rounded, touch-friendly interactive tokens:

- **Cards & Data Containers**: Structured using `rounded-lg` (0.75rem / 12px) for calm, orderly framing.
- **Input Fields & Text Areas**: Balanced at `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Badges, Grammatical Chips & Syntax Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to immediately distinguish parsed tokenized vocabulary from surrounding boxed content.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, `border-radius: 8px`. Hover shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus states introduce an outer ring of `2px solid #38BDF8`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background of `transparent` or `#14161B`, border of `1px solid #242833`, text color `#F1F5F9`. Hover transitions surface to `#181B22` and border to `#333A4A`.

### Grammatical Syntax Chips (Pill Badges)
Tokens are rendered as pill tags (`border-radius: 9999px`) utilizing low-opacity tints with high-contrast text and borders:
- **Subject Chip**: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`, text `#38BDF8`.
- **Auxiliary Chip**: Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`, text `#F59E0B`.
- **Verb Chip**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.35)`, text `#34D399`.
- **Complement Chip**: Background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.35)`, text `#A78BFA`.

### Interactive Parsing Cards
- Encapsulate parsed sentences with an overarching `#14161B` card, `1px solid #242833`.
- Internal words or syntax segments sit on interactive `#181B22` sub-pill targets that highlight with matching category colors when hovered or selected.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background `#14161B`, border `1px solid #242833`, text `#F1F5F9`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focused state transitions the border to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox borders use `#333A4A` on unchecked state, filling with `#2563EB` when active.
- Toggles utilize a `#14161B` track with a 1px border; the active thumb glides into an electric blue state.
