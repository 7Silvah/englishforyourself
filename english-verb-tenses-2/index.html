English Verb Tenses

In this section, you will find information about English tenses.

What are the 12 English verb tenses in English grammar?

When you read 12 it may seem as too much information; however, English verb tenses are not that difficult once you notice the structure and usage.

We will provide the information about each English tense with examples in an easy way so that you do not get bored.

  1. Simple present or present simple tense.
  2. Present continuous / progressive tense.
  3. Present perfect tense.
  4. Present perfect continuous / progressive tense.
  5. Simple past tense.
  6. Past continuous / progressive tense.
  7. Past perfect simple tense.
  8. Past perfect continuous / progressive tense.
  9. Future simple (will) and future simple (going to) tense.
  10. Future continuous / progressive tense.
  11. Future perfect tense.
  12. Future perfect continuous / progressive tense.
