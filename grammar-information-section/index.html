Grammar

You are now in the grammar section and you may be thinking that grammar it is boring, but in fact, grammar helps you to create sentences. To give you a simple English grammar definition: it is the secret that makes English make sense when you are talking with your friends or family.

Now, it may seem troublesome and difficult, but once you start to understand what each word means, you will recognize patterns in the language that will make it easier for you to enjoy learning English.

How can you teach yourself English grammar?

When you are learning grammar by yourself it may seem tricky, you may not know where to start. Don’t worry, we are here to help you and give you some suggestions:

  1. Try to be super curious: when you read or listen something in English, identify repetitive patterns.
  2. Learn English grammar step by step: read basic English grammar such as parts of the speech.
  3. Identify English grammar structures: mainly clauses, sentences and phrases.
  4. Practice: once you recognize simple terms and structures, you will be able to put into practice what you have learned.

How can you learn the English grammar topics step by step?

Englishforyourself.com gives you a predetermined order of grammar topics with charts. When you follow the lessons in that order, you will be escalating one step at a time to reach your desired learning goals. We provide the lessons in that way so that you can start learning basic English grammar.

How can you improve your grammar?

When you already know English grammar, you may not know what to do next. Well, you never stop learning, which means, you never stop improving your skills. So, keep up with English grammar by doing the following:

  1. Don’t skip reading.
  2. If you forget about a topic, read it again and make notes.
  3. Practice with people or PDF worksheets.
  4. Listen to different songs in English, not the usual ones you listen so that you notice new structures.

How can you learn English grammar for free?

There is no need for you to spend any money to learn English when there is already much information and many resources available for you.

Englishforyourself.com already provides you with everything you need to start your journey in this beautiful language. You can find English grammar exercises with answers in PDF format.

What is more, you can watch movies, TV series, listen to music or podcasts, read newspapers or magazines. Everything is closer to you than you think.
