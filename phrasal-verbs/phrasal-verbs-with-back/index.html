Phrasal Verbs with Back

In this section you will learn useful phrasal verbs with back in English.

Useful phrasal verbs with back

  1. To back away: to retreat backwards.
  2. To back down: to withdraw from a situation because of a change of mind.
  3. To back into something: to move a vehicle in reverse into something.
  4. To back off: to withdraw from a conflict
  5. To back out of something: to cancel a plan.
  6. To back up:
    • To defend someone.
    • To make a copy of data.
  7. To back onto something: to face the back of something.
