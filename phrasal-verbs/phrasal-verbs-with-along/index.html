Phrasal Verbs with Along

In this section you will learn useful phrasal verbs with along.

Useful phrasal verbs with along

  • To come along: something making progress.
  • To get along with: to have a good relationship with someone else.
  • To go along: to comply with something.
  • To happen along: to encounter someone by chance.
  • To move along: to continue moving forward
  • To pass along: to share information to someone else.
  • To play along with: to pretend to follow someone’s actions.
  • To sing along with: to join someone who is singing.
  • To tag along: to join a group even if you weren’t invited.
