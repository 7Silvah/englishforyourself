English Tenses Overview

In this section, you will find information about English tenses.

What are the 12 tenses in English grammar?

When you read 12, it may seem as too much information; however, English tenses are not that difficult once you learn the structure and usage. We will provide the information about each English tense with examples in an easy way so that you do not get bored.
