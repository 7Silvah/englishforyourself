Expressions to Show Concern

In this section you will learn expressions to show concern in English.

Ways to show concern in English:

  1. Are you alright?
  2. Are you feeling okay?
  3. Can I help in any way?
  4. Do you need a shoulder to cry on?
  5. Do you want to talk about it?
  6. Is everything alright?
  7. Is there anything I can do?
  8. What’s the matter?
  9. What’s wrong?
  10. Why the long face?
  11. You don’t seem like yourself today.
  12. You seem upset.
