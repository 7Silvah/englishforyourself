Expressions for Advice

In this section you will learn expressions to ask for advice and to give advice in English.

Asking for advice:

What should I do?
What do you suggest?
What would you advise me to do?
Any suggestions?
How do I approach this problem?

Giving advice:

I think you might want to…
How about…?
If I were you, I would…
Why don’t you…?
I think you should / ought to…
I would strongly suggest that…
Have you considered…?
I think it might be a good idea…
If I were in your shoes, I would…
Have you tried…?
Have you thought of…?
I think it’d be a good idea…
