Expressions for Agreeing and Disagreeing

In this section you will learn expressions for agreeing and disagreeing in English.

Expressions for agreeing:

  1. Absolutely!
  2. I agree with you.
  3. I couldn’t agree more.
  4. I feel the same.
  5. I share the same opinion.
  6. I think so too.
  7. That’s a good point.
  8. That’s all true.
  9. That’s exactly what I think.
  10. That’s true
  11. That’s true.
  12. We see eye to eye.
  13. We’re on the same page.
  14. You’re absolutely right.
  15. You’ve hit the nail on the head.

Expressions for disagreeing:

  1. I can’t share that view.
  2. I disagree.
  3. I don’t agree with you.
  4. I don’t think so.
  5. I doubt it.
  6. I see things differently.
  7. I see what you mean but…
  8. I’m afraid I disagree.
  9. I’m not sure about that.
  10. Respectfully, I don’t agree.
  11. That’s not entirely true.
  12. We’ll have to disagree.
